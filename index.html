A personal assistant for one person

Zyphos is a private assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows computer. It is not a public service, it isn't sold, and nobody else can sign up for it.

What it does

  • Talks with its owner by voice or text, in English and Spanish.
  • Keeps reminders, goals and notes on his computer.
  • With his permission, connects to his own Google account to sort new email, draft replies for his approval, and read and add calendar events.

How it uses Google

Zyphos asks Google for two permissions, for its owner's account only:

  • Gmail: to read and label new email, save drafts, and send, archive or move to Trash only after the owner says yes each time.
  • Google Calendar: to show today's events and add the events and reminders the owner asks for.

Email is read only by an AI model that runs on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any outside AI service, sold or shared. Details are in the privacy policy.
