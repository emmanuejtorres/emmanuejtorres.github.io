Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Zyphos is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on his own computer. This policy explains what it does with information from his Google account.

What Zyphos accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): new messages, labels and drafts in the owner's own mailbox.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): events on the owner's own calendars.

Zyphos only connects to the account its owner signs in with. It doesn't access anyone else's account.

How it's used

  • To sort new email, tell the owner what needs him, and write draft replies for him to approve.
  • To show and add calendar events and reminders he asks for.
  • Sending, archiving or moving email to Trash happens only after the owner approves each one.

Where it's kept

  • Email content is read by an AI model running on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any outside AI service.
  • Short summaries and sorting labels are stored on that computer only. The Google sign-in is stored in a private folder on that computer.
  • Email and calendar details are not passed to text-messaging apps. They stay on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Information from Google is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It isn't transferred to anyone.

Zyphos's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Zyphos's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored summaries from his computer.
